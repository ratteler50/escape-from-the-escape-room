# Prompt pack: character model sheets (slice 19, step 1)

Generate these in **ChatGPT** (image generation), one chat per character so earlier images don't
bleed into the next. Save each result into `art-inbox/` (git-ignored) with the file name given. If
ChatGPT offers several, pick your favourite; regenerate as often as you like. The agent takes it from
there.

**Attach to every prompt:** `art-inbox/REFERENCE-lair-background.png` (the painted Lair). It's for
**palette and mood only**: the characters must NOT be painted like it; they're flat colour with hard
outlines so they stand out against the painted rooms (decision 0005, rule 2).

**Size:** ask for **landscape** (1536×1024).

## 1. Dana Pickwick → save as `art-inbox/dana-sheet.png`

> Character model sheet for a 2D cartoon point-and-click adventure game, in the spirit of 1990s
> LucasArts games like The Curse of Monkey Island. Style: flat colour with simple two-tone cel
> shading and bold, clean dark outlines; exaggerated, playful cartoon proportions (slightly
> oversized head, hands and feet); expressive face. Use the attached painting only for its colour
> palette and mood: the character itself must be flat and outlined, not painted.
>
> Character: Dana Pickwick, 19, a trainee at a tacky fantasy escape-room franchise. Earnest, polite,

> trousers, and worn sneakers. Short, tousled hair in an androgynous cut; gender-neutral styling.
> Hopeful, slightly worried smile.
>
> Show the same character three times side by side, full body, standing, same scale: front view,
> three-quarter view facing right, and side view facing right. Plain flat light-grey background,
> no scenery, no shadows on the ground, no other characters, no text other than the badge.

### 2b. Recolour: Reginald's robe → save as `art-inbox/reginald-sheet-2.png`

A mock-up in the Lair showed his purple robe disappearing into the purple walls. In the **same chat**
as the sheet (or a new one with `art-inbox/reginald-sheet.webp` attached), ask for an edit, not a new
drawing:

> Edit this exact image. Keep everything identical: the four poses, the line work, his skull, crown,
> hands, expression, proportions, and the plain grey background. Change only the colours of his
> clothes: the robe and cape become near-black charcoal, the cape's inner lining and the robe's
> inner panels become deep crimson red, and keep all the gold trim and the gold skull ornaments
> exactly as they are. His boots become near-black with gold buckles. Remove the dark block he sits
> on in the fourth pose: he sits on nothing, with the same pose. Flat colour with bold outlines, as
> before.

If the edit drifts (a different face or pose), try once more with "do not redraw, recolour only". If
it still drifts, a new sheet with the section 2 prompt, where "dark-purple robe" becomes "near-black
robe with a deep crimson lining", is fine too.
