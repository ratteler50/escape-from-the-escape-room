# The Lich's Lair background

`public/assets/rooms/lair.webp`, 1280×720. Generated 2026-10-22 with Canva's image generation
(image-to-image), recipe A2 from decision 0005. Two candidates were generated; the developer chose
**candidate 2** at full size (the brighter one, with candles either side of the throne).

**References:**
1. Layout: `docs/art/lair-layout.svg`, rendered to 1280×720 PNG (every prop at its hotspot's bounds,
   plus the spotlight rig aimed at Gary and Reginald's taped mark in the dark).
2. Style: the approved A2 prototype image (from the art-style prototype, since deleted:
   `docs/prototypes/lair-art-style.md`).

**Prompt:**

> Video game background, hand-painted 1990s cartoon point-and-click adventure style: lush painterly
> brushwork, rich saturated colours, warm torchlight against cool purple shadows; crooked,
> exaggerated cartoon proportions, leaning walls, wobbly lines, nothing perfectly straight, playful
> and goofy rather than spooky; clear silhouettes and uncluttered empty wall areas so interactive
> objects stand out from the background. Match the painting style, palette and lighting of the
> second reference image exactly. Scene: a tacky, low-budget "Lich's Lair" room inside a cheap
> escape-room franchise, comedic rather than scary: a few fake cobwebs, plastic candles, a price
> sticker here and there. Follow the FIRST reference image's layout exactly, keeping every object in
> the same position and size: on the far left an upright wooden coffin; next to it a plastic skeleton
> on a stand, brightly lit by a theatre spotlight; the spotlight is an old stage lamp mounted high on
> the wall above the middle-left, its beam cutting diagonally down onto the skeleton; on the stone
> floor in front of the throne a taped yellow X mark sits in darkness, unlit; just left of the throne
> a small glowing green skull-shaped jar on a low wooden plinth; in the centre an empty, over-the-top
> bone throne; right of it a small glass display case on a wooden pedestal; above that a cork board
> covered in pinned customer review cards; a small electronic keypad on the wall; on the right an
> arched wooden dungeon door; on the stone floor in front a dark metal floor grate. The floor fills
> the lower part of the image. No people, no characters, no readable text, no UI.

**Pipeline:** placed full-page in the Canva design "Escape-O-Rama: Lich's Lair" (folder
"Escape-O-Rama assets"; candidate 1 on page 1, candidate 2 on page 2), exported as a lossless
1280×720 PNG, converted with `cwebp -q 85` (83 KB).
