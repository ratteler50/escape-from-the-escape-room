# Prompt pack: the open coffin (slice 33)

Reginald's coffin opens in slice 33: the inside shows his "dressing room", with his script and his
hairspray as things to click. Two edits of the **same** crop make it: the coffin open with the script
inside (1), and the same picture with the script taken out (2). The agent turns them into an "open"
patch and a small script patch, drawn only while the script is in the coffin, the same way as the
display case's scroll (`prompts-overlays.md` §3).

As with the other overlays, you edit an **enlarged crop** of the painting, not the whole room. Attach
the crop, ask for an **edit** (same size and framing), and save the result under the name given. If
ChatGPT redraws the whole crop (different colours, lighting or angle), try again with "do not
redraw; change only what I describe".

The crop already has the new gold star on the lid (drawn by the agent): keep it.

---

## 1. The coffin open → attach `art-inbox/CROP-coffin.png`, then `art-inbox/ICON-script.png`; save as `art-inbox/coffin-open.png`

> Edit the first image. Keep everything identical: framing, size, painting style, colours, the warm
> candlelight, the purple wall and cobwebs, and the floor. Change only one thing: the upright wooden
> coffin is now OPEN. Its lid is hinged on its LEFT edge and swung open towards the viewer, so the
> lid is seen almost edge-on along the left side, with the brass knocker ring and the gold star
> still on its outside. Inside, the coffin is lined with faded red velvet and set up as an actor's
> tiny dressing room:
>
> - near the top, a small round mirror ringed with little light bulbs;
> - pinned below the mirror, a signed photo of a skeletal lich in a crooked gold crown;
> - on a little shelf low down on the LEFT, a dog-eared stack of paper bound with a red ribbon
>   (an actor's script, like the second image);
> - on a little shelf low down on the RIGHT, well apart from the script, a tall spray can of
>   hairspray with a bright green label.
>
> Keep the script and the hairspray clearly separate and easy to see. No people. No readable text
> except what is already in the picture.

## 2. Without the script → in the **same chat**, save as `art-inbox/coffin-open-no-script.png`

> Keep this exact image: the open coffin, the lid, the mirror, the photo, the hairspray, the
> lighting, everything. Change only one thing: remove the stack of paper with the red ribbon from
> the left-hand shelf, leaving the empty shelf and the velvet behind it.
